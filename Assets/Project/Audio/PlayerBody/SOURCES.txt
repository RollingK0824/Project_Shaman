Player Body Audio — source notes

Footsteps: reuse existing five Unity Starter Assets ThirdPersonController WAVs.
Source assets and importer settings were not changed. These are existing licensed project assets;
retain the Unity Asset Store/Starter Assets license when distributing the game. No new web downloads.

Heartbeat_Pulse_Placeholder.wav: original procedural synthesis (two damped low-frequency pulses).
Breathing_Loop_Placeholder.wav: 48 kHz interpolation of the project's original synthesized
Stress_Breathing.wav (documented in PlayerGameplay/Stress/README.md); not a human recording.
Both are mono 48 kHz 16-bit PCM, without clipping. No third-party audio was used for synthesis.
Both body clips are PLACEHOLDERS. In particular, replace synthetic breathing with an authorized
human recording for natural final quality. Regenerating/resampling does not make it a recording.

SoundManager/MainMixer are unchanged. PlayerAudioController routes persistent sources to SFX.
Ground types are not classified consistently; a single footstep set is used, without a new framework.
